Definitions & data notes

Units include Qty + Bounce, matching the current workbook convention. Sales value follows the source workbook. Ebdaa is included. Value targets use Target2 units × the target-sheet price, pending approval. The Period and Data view controls set actual sales everywhere. Target period changes targets and achievement only. Monthly performance always uses monthly targets and ignores the global Target period. Negative sales adjustments remain included. Targets are product-level; distributor/channel selections show sales only. Fractional units are retained in calculations. Ranked charts compare actual units or EGP values. Breakdown percentages use total selected sales after filters, for either units or value. Portfolio company shares retain total company sales for the same period as their denominator. Target-gap percentages use the selected total shortfall. Selected months are combined once each; period targets cover only those months. Gaps in a month selection are not treated as zero sales.
