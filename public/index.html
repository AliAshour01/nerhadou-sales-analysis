Definitions and target assumptions

Units include Qty + Bounce, matching the current workbook convention. Sales value follows the source workbook. Ebdaa is included. Value targets use Target2 units × the target-sheet price, pending approval. The Period and Data view controls set actual sales everywhere. Target basis changes targets and achievement only. Monthly performance always uses monthly targets and ignores the global Target basis. Negative sales adjustments remain included. Targets are product-level; distributor/channel selections show sales only. Fractional units are retained in calculations. Ranked charts compare actual units or EGP values. Breakdown percentages use total selected sales after filters, for either units or value. Portfolio company shares retain total company sales for the same period as their denominator. Target-gap percentages use the selected total shortfall. Selected months are combined once each; period targets cover only those months. Gaps in a month selection are not treated as zero sales.

Connecting…

Add or update monthly sales

Prepare a template, fill Units and/or Value, then paste it below and preview. Existing periods are updated; new periods are created.

Change the active reporting range

For example, show a year only through June. Later actual records and full-year actual controls are retained but set aside. Targets are unchanged. Extend the range to restore existing data; no future sales are invented.

Paste structured data

CSV, Excel tab-separated text, or JSON. Units only and value only are supported. New products or distributors can be included by name and approved in the preview.

Full company replacement: old sales rows missing from your new data will be removed for these months. Blank Units/Value become unknown. Targets and stock stay unchanged.

Update mode: blank cells keep existing figures; omitted rows stay unchanged.
